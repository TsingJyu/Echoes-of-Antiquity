學者簡介Scholar Profile

蔡宗齊 教授Prof. Zong-qi Cai

蔡宗齊Prof. Zong-qi Cai

蔡宗齊教授現任美國伊利諾伊州立大學香檳校區東亞語言文化系及比較文學系教授，並兼任香港嶺南大學中文系講座教授。他在古典詩歌、文學理論、比較文學、美學、比較哲學、經學和佛教等領域有著深入的研究和豐富的學術成果。

Professor Zong-qi Cai currently serves as Professor of East Asian Languages and Cultures and Comparative Literature at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, and holds the concurrent position of Chair Professor of Chinese at Lingnan University, Hong Kong. He possesses profound academic expertise and extensive publications in classical Chinese poetry, literary theory, comparative literature, aesthetics, comparative philosophy, Confucian classics, and Buddhism.

《唐詩之音韻》英文版The Phonology of Tang Poetry (English)

蔡教授使用英文，為國際觀眾系統講解唐詩的音韻特點、格律結構（如五言、七言律詩構建）以及閱讀技巧，並巧妙融入了中西思維模式對比（如十四行詩與律詩）等跨文化元素。

Professor Cai systematically explains the phonological features, metrical structures (such as five- and seven-character regulated verse), and reading techniques of Tang poetry in English. He cleverly integrates cross-cultural elements, including comparisons between Chinese and Western poetic forms like sonnets and regulated verse.

探索與延伸：中華大地文旅Exploration: Cultural Tourism

文旅聯動試播Tourism Pilot

微博試播情況Weibo Pilot Broadcast

目前在微博平台試播了《唐詩深度遊》短視頻系列。該系列利用現有學術視頻素材進行了二次剪輯，其中結合香港伶仃洋實地取景的《過零丁洋》篇已正式播出，作為「詩詞+文旅」模式的初步嘗試。

Currently piloting the "In-Depth Tour of Tang Poetry" short video series on Weibo. The series utilizes existing academic video footage for re-editing, and the episode "Crossing Lingdingyang," featuring on-site footage from Lingdingyang in Hong Kong, has officially aired as an initial attempt at the "Poetry + Tourism" model.

實地素材儲備Field Footage Reserves

除了已播出的香港伶仃洋素材，節目組已前往武漢黃鶴樓完成了《登黃鶴樓》的核心實景畫面拍攝。目前該素材暫未剪輯釋出，計劃尋找更優質的視聽平台與製作資源進行匹配發酵。

In addition to the aired Lingdingyang footage, the team has traveled to the Yellow Crane Tower in Wuhan to complete the core location shooting for "Climbing the Yellow Crane Tower." This footage is currently unedited, pending partnerships with premium audiovisual platforms and production resources.

後續尋訪計劃Future Expeditions

該項目後續計劃探訪長江篇的岳陽樓（《登岳陽樓》）、襄陽漢江（《漢江臨眺》），以及塞外篇的甘肅武威（《涼州詞》）。期望能借助更專業的平台力量，將學術積澱轉化為深度的文旅視聽節目。

Future plans include visiting Yueyang Tower and the Han River in Xiangyang for the Yangtze River chapter, as well as Wuwei in Gansu for the Frontier chapter. The goal is to leverage professional platforms to transform academic heritage into profound cultural tourism programs.
